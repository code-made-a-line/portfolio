My interest in technology started at a young age. I was always the go-to person in my family for anything computer or tech-related, whether it was setting up devices, troubleshooting problems, navigating online services, filling out forms, or figuring out new apps. I also loved exploring the family computer, downloading just about anything I could find (sometimes giving it a virus or two in the process...oops).
