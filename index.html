// about · my story so far

I like making confusing things simple.

Most of my work, at my job, in tutoring and in class, comes down to the same thing: understanding how a system works, then explaining it so someone else can use it. That's what drew me to computer science, and it's what I want to keep doing after graduation.

  1. Teaching Excel, in two languages

    Volunteering as an Excel tutor at Erie Neighborhood House, I wrote my own lesson plans and switched between English and Spanish to fit each student. It was the first time I saw how much a clear explanation matters.

  2. Payroll intern to Payroll Assistant

    I joined After School Matters as a payroll intern in 2024 and was promoted a year later. I've resolved 5,000+ tickets, tracked every lost and reissued payment until it was right, and now train new hires and help interview interns.

  3. Building, not just using, the tools

    Through my CS degree and an app development internship at Duct Tape Games, I moved from spreadsheets to Python, SQL, C# and hardware: debugging a Unity game and building a Flask dashboard for a conveyor belt sorter.

  4. What's next

    After I graduate in May 2027, I want a role where I help people and support the processes that keep a team running, like operations, payroll/HR or technical support, with room to use my CS skills to make those processes better.
